# Portfolio case study

Audience: potential freelance clients (small-business owners and agency leads), not engineers. They skim.

Produce `docs/CASE_STUDY.md` (300–500 words):
1. **The problem**: one paragraph in plain language. Who has it, why existing tools fall short (e.g., trust concerns about third-party Drive tools).
2. **What I built**: 3–5 bullets of user-visible capabilities, with a screenshot/GIF placeholder.
3. **How**: the short version of the architecture, and why it's safe (no backend, token stays in the browser, open source).
4. **Hard parts**: 2–3 real challenges from the notes (e.g., recursive folder copy, rate limits) and how they were solved.
5. **Result**: links (Web Store, GitHub), tests and CI status, what it would take to adapt for a client.

Also update README.md (badges, screenshot, install, 60-second quick start) and draft `docs/store-listing.md` (short description ≤132 chars, full description, privacy-practices answers).

Be honest: mention the AI-assisted workflow in the "How" section as a strength. Never invent metrics or users.
